The AI app ecosystem is where mobile apps were in 2008. ChatGPT has 900 million weekly active users, but only ~162 apps in its store. The window for early movers is wide open — and you don't need engineers to walk through it.

## The Problem with Traditional AI Development

Building an MCP server from scratch requires:

- Backend engineering (Node.js or Python)
- Understanding MCP protocol specifications
- Authentication and security implementation
- Widget rendering for rich UI
- Deployment and hosting infrastructure
- Monitoring and analytics

For most businesses, this means 4-8 weeks of engineering time and $20,000-50,000 in development costs. That's a non-starter for testing a new distribution channel.

## The No-Code Alternative

Visual builders like [Drio](https://www.getdrio.com/blog/build-ai-apps-without-code) are changing this equation. Instead of writing code, you:

1. **Connect your APIs** — drag and drop your existing endpoints
2. **Design the experience** — choose from widget primitives (cards, forms, charts, maps)
3. **Set the conversation flow** — define how the AI interacts with users
4. **Publish** — one-click deployment to ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini

Total time: 5-10 minutes. Cost: a fraction of custom development.

## What Can You Build?

The use cases span every industry:

- **E-commerce**: Product discovery, comparison shopping, checkout inside chat
- **Healthcare**: Symptom checkers, appointment booking, health assessments
- **Real Estate**: Property search, mortgage calculators, virtual tours
- **Travel**: Trip planning, booking, itinerary management
- **Insurance**: Quote comparison, claims filing, policy management

## The First-Mover Advantage

Every app store in history has rewarded early entrants. The companies that build apps now will have months of usage data, user feedback, and optimization when AI discovery turns on at scale.
