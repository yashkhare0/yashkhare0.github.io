OpenAI quietly launched an app store inside ChatGPT. With 900 million weekly active users, it's already one of the largest digital platforms on Earth — and it has fewer than 200 apps. For comparison, Apple's App Store had 500 apps at launch for 6 million iPhone users.

The math is staggering: 4.5 million potential users per app on ChatGPT, versus 12,000 per app on the original App Store.

## How ChatGPT Apps Work

ChatGPT apps are powered by the Model Context Protocol (MCP). When a user interacts with an app, ChatGPT:

1. Identifies the relevant app based on the conversation
2. Calls the app's MCP server with structured tool requests
3. Receives data and UI components back
4. Renders rich, interactive experiences inline in the chat

Users can browse apps, add them to their workspace, and interact with them conversationally. It's a fundamentally different interaction model than websites or mobile apps.

## Why Businesses Should Pay Attention

AI agents are already driving over 1 billion visits to businesses monthly. The companies that have apps in ChatGPT are capturing this traffic. The ones that don't are invisible.

Consider the automotive industry: AutoScout24, CarGurus, CarMax, and CARS24 all have ChatGPT apps. When someone asks ChatGPT "find me a used BMW in Berlin," these companies show up. Everyone else doesn't.
